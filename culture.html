Telangana

Discover the vibrant traditions, colorful festivals, artistic heritage, dance, food and folk music of Telangana.

The Spirit of Telangana

Telangana is a land of rich traditions and vibrant cultural expressions. From the colorful celebration of Bathukamma to the elegant Pochampally Ikat, the state preserves a unique heritage passed down through generations.

🌸 Bathukamma

Bathukamma is a colorful floral festival celebrated by women across Telangana. Flowers are arranged in beautiful layers to create a distinctive flower stack, around which women sing and dance traditional songs.

🧵 Pochampally Ikat

Pochampally Ikat is a famous Telangana handloom tradition known for its geometric patterns and distinctive tie-and-dye weaving technique. It is closely associated with the town of Bhoodan Pochampally.

💃 Perini Dance

Perini Shivathandavam is a traditional dance form associated with Telangana. Historically performed by warriors, it is known for its energetic movements, powerful rhythm and connection with Lord Shiva.

🍛 Traditional Food

Telangana cuisine is known for bold flavors and spicy dishes. Popular foods include Sarva Pindi, Sakinalu, Jonna Rotte, Pachi Pulusu, Hyderabadi Biryani and various traditional pickles and chutneys.

🎶 Folk Music

Telangana has a rich folk music tradition featuring lively songs, rhythmic performances and instruments. Oggu Katha is one of the well-known storytelling traditions combining music, narration and performance.
